1. Reduce, Reuse, & Recycle

This is one of the best ways to take care of the planet. Before you throw something away, try to reuse it or find a new way to use it. Always recycle paper, glass, and plastic when you can.The three Rs stand for Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle, which is a simple guide to protecting our planet. Reducing is the most important step because it means buying less and creating less waste in the first place. Reusing means finding new ways to use old items instead of throwing them away. Recycling is the final step, where materials like paper, glass, and plastic are collected and turned into completely new products.

2. Save Clean Water

Water is very important for all living things. We must not waste it. You can help by turning off the water tap while you brush your teeth and by taking shorter showers every day.

3. Protect Plants and Trees

Trees give us clean air to breathe and homes for animals. We can protect our land by planting new flowers and trees. It also helps to pick up trash so our parks stay clean.
